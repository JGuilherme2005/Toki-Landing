## What feels off and the intended response

1. **The hero presents a feature inventory before an experience.** At [375](../screenshots/polish-v2/before/live-375.png) and [1280](../screenshots/polish-v2/before/live-1280.png), two headline sentences, a long product list, three links and four trust labels all precede the first screenshot. [Forest](../screenshots/polish-v2/references/forest-hero.png) and [Opal](../screenshots/polish-v2/references/opal-hero.png) lead with one memorable line and a single clear next step. Aim for one line, a short explanation, one Windows CTA, and a quiet platform note.
2. **The strongest brand asset arrives too late.** The reading frog is visible only in the mobile Deep Focus screen well below the desktop story in the [375 full page](../screenshots/polish-v2/before/live-375.png). [Finch](../screenshots/polish-v2/references/finch-hero.png) makes its character immediately recognizable; [Forest](../screenshots/polish-v2/references/forest-feature.png) uses its character to explain the experience. Use the actual frog reading pond video in the hero, then let the mascot reappear only in the mobile section.
3. **The product images are present but hard to read as proof.** On the [375 capture](../screenshots/polish-v2/before/live-375.png), the desktop windows shrink to thumbnail scale; the timer and blocker states are barely legible. [Linear](../screenshots/polish-v2/references/linear-feature.png) gives a real interface a large, quiet frame and generous margins. Use the new native screenshots at larger sizes, show the running timer and a selected blocked site, and caption the state rather than adding another card list.
4. **Every section uses nearly the same pattern.** Audio, blocker, integrations, continuity, streak and privacy repeat a headline, paragraph, three bordered points, and a screenshot in the [1280 full page](../screenshots/polish-v2/before/live-1280.png). [Session's feature](../screenshots/polish-v2/references/session-feature.png) varies scale with a single large product scene and short text. Give each key feature one visual job and remove redundant feature cards.
5. **The page asks for too much reading before the download.** The four-step cards, five-item value strip, many feature lists, mobile status card, nine-row comparison, privacy cards, pricing card and FAQ make the [375 page](../screenshots/polish-v2/before/live-375.png) unusually long. [Endel](../screenshots/polish-v2/references/endel-hero.png) gives its product story open space between a small amount of copy and a strong visual. Compress repeated explanations, retain the verified platform differences in a concise compare/details area, and preserve the factual FAQ.
6. **The visual rhythm is dense despite large section gaps.** The [1280 page](../screenshots/polish-v2/before/live-1280.png) moves from tightly packed small cards to long stretches of dark background. [Linear](../screenshots/polish-v2/references/linear-hero.png) uses a consistent text width, screenshot edge, and section spacing. Establish a steadier content grid, subtler 1 px borders, and more deliberate image framing.
7. **Secondary text and choices lose hierarchy.** Kicker text, trust indicators, thumbnail detail and repeated green outlines are very small in the [375 page](../screenshots/polish-v2/before/live-375.png). The Windows CTA competes with an Android beta link before Android availability has been explained. [Session](../screenshots/polish-v2/references/session-hero.png) separates platform availability from the main product proposition. Keep the Windows download dominant, label Android private beta plainly, and ensure useful text and focus states meet AA contrast.

## Iteration record

- [Round 1 at 375](../screenshots/polish-v2/round-1/375.png) / [1440](../screenshots/polish-v2/round-1/1440.png): the video and quieter frame established the direction, but the value strip and four-step cards delayed product proof and kept the page dense.
- [Round 2 at 375](../screenshots/polish-v2/round-2/375.png) / [1440](../screenshots/polish-v2/round-2/1440.png): removed those repeated sections and moved the blocker next to the hero. The phone section and separate comparison still repeated a long platform story.
- [Round 3 at 375](../screenshots/polish-v2/round-3/375.png) / [1440](../screenshots/polish-v2/round-3/1440.png): enlarged narrow-screen desktop evidence, moved the comparison next to mobile status, and made detailed mobile features opt-in. The Android private-beta and no-blocking caveats remain visible.

## Constraints for implementation

Keep the verified product claims and real installer destination. Android remains a private beta with no public store download; mobile blocking is unavailable. Do not add testimonials, invented metrics, or fake UI. Use Syne for titles, the system body face, and JetBrains Mono for numbers. Keep the existing moonlit pond palette: `#111513`, `#171c19`, `#efe9dc`, `#a3a79b`, `#8fc27a`, `#f0c75e`.
