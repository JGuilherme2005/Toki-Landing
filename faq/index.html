GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

The details that help you decide whether Sylviae fits your day.

What is Sylviae?

A calm focus workspace. The Windows beta combines a timer, tasks, distraction blocking, focus audio, and task imports. An Android companion is in private beta.

Is Sylviae free?

The current Windows v1.0.0 beta is free to download and use. No account is required for its core tools.

Is it just a Pomodoro timer?

No. Sylviae keeps your task context, distraction controls, and focus audio beside the timer.

Does it work offline?

Core timer, local tasks, history, blocker preferences, and offline tracks stay on your device. Cloud imports and streaming audio need an internet connection.

Which devices are supported?

The public installer supports Windows 64-bit. Android is a private beta by request, with no public mobile download or store listing. There is no macOS, Linux, or iOS release.

Does Android block sites and apps?

Not yet. The Android beta saves your blocklist only. Site and app blocking is a Windows desktop feature.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the core Windows tools. Android can be used without an account. Connected integrations may ask you to sign in to those services.

Why does Windows ask for permission?

To enforce website blocking at the system level, Windows may ask for a one-time permission.

Are the desktop screenshots the current download?

The native desktop images are previews. The public installer is the Windows v1.0.0 beta, a separate current build.
