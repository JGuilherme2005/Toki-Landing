GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

The details that help you decide whether Sylviae fits your day.

What is Sylviae?

A calm focus workspace for Windows, iOS, and Android combining a timer, tasks, distraction blocking, focus audio, and Deep Focus mode.

Is Sylviae free?

Yes. Sylviae is free to download and use on Windows, iOS, and Android. No account is required for its core tools.

Is it just a Pomodoro timer?

No. Sylviae keeps your task context, distraction controls, and focus audio beside the timer.

Does it work offline?

Core timer, local tasks, history, blocker preferences, and offline tracks stay on your device. Cloud imports and streaming audio need an internet connection.

Which devices are supported?

Sylviae is available for Windows 64-bit on the Microsoft Store or direct download from sylviae.space, and for mobile on the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

What is included in the mobile app?

The iOS and Android apps include the focus timer, tasks, Deep Focus pond, focus audio, history, streaks, and your saved blocklist, while system-level site and app blocking runs on Windows desktop.

Do I need an account?

No account is required for the core Windows or mobile tools. If you create an account, your tasks, focus history, and saved blocklist can sync across signed-in devices. Connected integrations may ask you to sign in to those services.

Why does Windows ask for permission?

To enforce website blocking at the system level, Windows may ask for a one-time permission.
