THE WINDOWS WORKSPACE

Make room for focus.

A timer, one task, a quieter screen. The current v1.0.0 beta is free for Windows 64-bit.

Download the Windows beta ↓
01 / FOCUS

One task at a time.

Start a focus timer beside your local tasks. Your next task stays close without pulling you into another app.

The native desktop image is a preview. The download is the current Windows v1.0.0 beta.

02 / BLOCKING

A boundary that holds.

Choose distracting websites and applications. On Windows, Sylviae keeps the selected sites and apps out of reach during a focus session.

Blocking is a Windows desktop feature. Android currently saves your blocklist without enforcing it.

03 / SOUNDS & DEEP FOCUS

Sound that follows your time.

Choose offline focus music or save Spotify and YouTube sources. Audio can follow the timer, fall silent during breaks, or keep playing.

Deep Focus turns the session into a quieter full-screen pond, with your task and countdown in view.

These native desktop images are previews; the current v1.0.0 Windows beta download is a separate build.
