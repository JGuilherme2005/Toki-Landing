A few good things to know.

Is Sylviae free?

Yes. Sylviae is free to download and use on Windows. No account is required for the core tools.

Does it work offline?

The core timer, local tasks, history, blocker preferences, and offline tracks stay on your device. Cloud imports and streaming audio need internet.

Which devices are supported?

Sylviae is available for Windows 64-bit as a direct download on this site. An Android preview is in testing.

Does mobile include focus tools and blocklists?

Yes. The Android preview includes the focus timer, tasks, Deep Focus mode, focus audio, history, streaks, and your saved blocklist, while system-level site and app blocking runs on Windows desktop.
