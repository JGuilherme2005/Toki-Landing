A few good things to know.

Is Sylviae free?

Yes. Sylviae is free to download and use on Windows, iOS, and Android. No account is required for the core tools. If you create an account, your focus data can sync across signed-in devices.

Does it work offline?

The core timer, local tasks, history, blocker preferences, and offline tracks stay on your device. Cloud imports and streaming audio need internet.

Which devices are supported?

Sylviae is available for Windows 64-bit via the Microsoft Store or direct download on this site, and on mobile via the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

Does mobile include focus tools and blocklists?

Yes. The mobile app includes the focus timer, tasks, Deep Focus mode, focus audio, history, streaks, and your saved blocklist, while system-level site and app blocking runs on Windows desktop.
