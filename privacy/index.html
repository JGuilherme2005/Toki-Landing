LOCAL BY DEFAULT

Your focus stays yours.

You can use the core timer, tasks, history, blocker preferences, and offline tracks without an account.

01 / ON DEVICE

Start without an account.

Core Windows tools work locally. Without an account, your tasks and session history stay on your device.

02 / WHEN YOU CONNECT

If you create an account.

Your tasks, focus history, and saved blocklist can sync across your signed-in devices. Cloud imports and streaming audio contact their services when you choose to use them. Windows integration credentials use protected local storage.

03 / QUIET BY DESIGN

No attention market.

Sylviae is not built around advertising. When site analytics are enabled, Plausible counts page visits and Windows download clicks; it does not show ads.
