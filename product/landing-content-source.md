## 4. Concise Feature Descriptions

### Core Pomodoro Timer
> **Structure your work with calm precision.**  
> Toki’s Pomodoro engine manages work sessions, short breaks, and long breaks with zero timer drift. Custom durations, automated cycle transitions, and wall-clock sleep recovery keep your routine consistent across system sleeps and window minimizes.

### Deep Focus Audio & Soundscapes
> **Sound that moves with your work.**  
> Pair your focus sessions with packaged offline ambient soundscapes or stream directly from Spotify and YouTube. In timer-bound mode, your audio starts automatically when focus begins and fades softly when your session completes.

### Desktop Distraction Blocker
> **Enforce focus at the desktop level.**  
> Keep chosen websites and applications out of reach while your focus timer runs. Toki uses system-level process management and hosts DNS blocking to close distracting apps and browser tabs automatically without browser extensions.

### Unified Workspace & Daily Tasks
> **Focus on what matters today.**  
> Manage daily task lists directly beside your timer. Set active focus tasks, organize daily routines that persist across sessions, and let Toki automatically clear yesterday’s completed items at midnight.

### Connected Integrations
> **Bring your tasks into focus.**  
> Import scheduled calendar events and tasks from Google Calendar, Todoist, and Notion with one click. Toki deduplicates imported items and automatically updates Notion database statuses as you complete your work.

### Local Analytics & Streak History
> **Track consistency, not vanity metrics.**  
> Review daily focus time, session history, and current streaks on a clean monthly calendar grid. All analytics run 100% locally in-memory without remote data collection.

## 5. Integration Descriptions

- **Google Calendar:** Import scheduled meetings and timeblocks directly as focus tasks. Filter events by timeframe (today, next 7 days, next 30 days) with automatic duplicate detection. *(1-Way Import)*
- **Notion:** Import unchecked task checkboxes from Notion pages and databases. Toggling task completion in Toki automatically updates your Notion page status, completion timestamps, and focus minutes. *(2-Way Sync)*
- **Todoist:** Connect your Todoist account to select projects and pull uncompleted tasks into Toki with priority tags and due dates. *(1-Way Import)*

## 6. Privacy, Security, & Offline Copy

### Local-First Guarantee
> **Your data stays on your PC.**  
> Toki is built local-first. Your tasks, timer history, streaks, and settings are saved directly to your local device. Toki contains zero telemetry, zero analytics tracking, and no external user accounts.

### Protected Integration Security
> **Secure OAuth without credential exposure.**  
> External integrations use Cloudflare Worker brokers and OS-backed Windows `safeStorage` DPAPI encryption. Third-party API tokens are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and never touch local disk or renderer script memory unencrypted.

### Offline & Online Boundaries
> **Runs offline when you need it.**  
> The core Pomodoro timer, workspace tasks, distraction blocker, and built-in ambient soundscapes run 100% offline without an internet connection. Network connection is only required when syncing Notion/Todoist/Google Calendar or streaming Spotify/YouTube audio.
